Melting Waveforms Artist

Lyrae Nova

K-Pop Inspired • Synthwave • Electronic Pop

Lyrae Nova grew up in New York City and traded it for Los Angeles without looking back. She came for the music and stayed for everything else. The lights, the people, the specific feeling of a city that runs on ambition and doesn't apologize for it.

Her sound is bright, crisp and immediately infectious. K-pop inspired electronic production with a clear high vocal that cuts through everything. She's enthusiastic without being naive, confident without being cold, and honest in the way that only someone still figuring things out can be.

Lyrae doesn't fill a room. She lights it up.

You move before you mean to.
