The Night She Got Out

Harland Wexler

Harland was fifteen when his family quietly fell apart — his mother's health failing, his stepfather's gambling finally coming to light at the same time. For five years he held everything together. When his mother was finally well enough to leave, it was Harland who made sure she did. He never called it brave. He just called it Tuesday.

Scene — Kaelith

Before The Sardonic

Kaelith

She loved someone fully once. He met someone else and kept it quiet. Kaelith — the one who always sees through everything — was the last one to see what was happening in her own relationship. She watched the other girl perform for him and saw right through the act. She just didn't know yet what the act was covering. By the time she did, she had already built the walls. The sardonic precision the world knows now was constructed carefully, deliberately, from something that used to be wide open. Anomalies was where she stopped looking inward and started looking outward. Everything after that was different.
